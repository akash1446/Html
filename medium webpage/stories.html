One day, a student named Ravi was struggling with his homework. He tried to read his book, but the lesson was hard to understand. Feeling confused, he opened his computer and asked ChatGPT for help. “Can you explain this in a simple way?” Ravi asked. ChatGPT replied with clear steps and examples. Ravi read them slowly, and suddenly the topic made sense. He smiled and tried solving the problem again—this time, he got the right answer!From that day on, whenever Ravi had doubts, he asked questions, learned new things, and improved every day. He realized that ChatGPT was like a helpful study partner—always ready to guide him, anytime he needed.Moral: Asking questions is the first step to learning, and the right help can make difficult things easy.
